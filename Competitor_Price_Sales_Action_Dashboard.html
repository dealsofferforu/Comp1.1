Core Metrics

Price DifferenceOur Price − Competitor Price (₹). Positive = we are dearer.
Price Gap %((Our Price − Competitor Price) / Competitor Price) × 100. Blank when competitor price is missing/zero.
Sales MultipleCompetitor Sales / Our Sales. Shows New/No Sales when our sales are zero (never Infinity).
Rank GapOur Rank − Competitor Rank. Positive = competitor ranks better (lower rank number is better).
Rating GapCompetitor Rating − Our Rating.
Review MultipleCompetitor Reviews / Our Reviews.
Content Gap+1 each: competitor has A+ (we don't), has video (we don't), or more images than us.
Total StockFC Stock + Warehouse Stock.
Stock DaysDays of cover from the source sheet — drives the stock-risk band and price guardrails.

Competitor Strength Score (out of 100)

Weights Monthly Sales 30 Sales Rank 20 Reviews 15 Rating 10 Price 10 A+ 5 Video 5 Images 5
RulesHigher sales, rating, reviews → higher score. Lower sales rank & lower price → higher score. Reviews use log scaling. A+/Video treated case-insensitively (yes/true/1). Scores are computed relative to competitors mapped to the same Our ASIN; missing values count as zero.

Benchmark Competitor Selection

Primary ruleThe competitor with the highest strength score for that Our ASIN — not automatically the cheapest.
ExcludedMissing competitor ASIN or price, mostly-blank competitor data, or competitor ASIN equal to our ASIN.
Tie-breakersWhen strength scores are close: higher monthly sales → better sales rank → stronger review count → price closest to our current price.

Price Action Logic

Low Stock (override)Stock < 15 days: do not reduce — maintain price, replenish; if < 7 days a 3–5% increase is allowed. Overrides all reduction rules.
Reduce PriceWe are >5% above a stronger benchmark and stock > 30 days. Reduction band by cover: 31–60 → 3%, 61–120 → 5%, >120 → 8%. Staged (not matched) when competitor is >20% below us.
Match CompetitorWe are within 5% above a stronger benchmark and stock > 30 days → test at benchmark price.
Increase PriceWe are >10% below benchmark, our rank matches/beats it, sales healthy, stock < 20 days → +3% (never above benchmark on first test).
Maintain PricePrice already competitive and competitor data is weak → hold and focus on stock/content.
Price Is Not the ProblemWe are at/below a benchmark that outsells and out-ranks us → fix rating, reviews, A+, video, images or FC stock instead of cutting price.
Test price roundingSnapped to customer-friendly endings in 9 (₹199, ₹249, ₹299 …).
